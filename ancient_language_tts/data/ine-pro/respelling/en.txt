These onsets are one onset. They do not add a syllable: ky, gy, kw, gw, bh, dh, gh, gyh, gwh. dh here is the aspirated stop. Do not use th for it.

The syllabic resonants are entire syllables, with no extra vowel beside them: /m̩/ em, /n̩/ en, /l̩/ el, /r̩/ er.

/ʔ/ is not written. /χ/ is kh. /ʁ/ is gh. /ə/ is uh.

Vowels
- /e/ → eh
- /eː/ → ey
- /o/ → aw
- /oː/ → oh
- /ɑ/ /ɑː/ → ah
- /i/ /iː/ → ee
- /u/ → uh
- /uː/ → oo
- /ei/ → ey
- /oi/ → oy
- /ɑi/ → eye
- /eu/ → eh'oo, one syllable
- /ou/ → aw'oo, one syllable
- /ɑu/ → ow
