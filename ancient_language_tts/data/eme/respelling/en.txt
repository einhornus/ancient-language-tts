The original spelling looks like modern English. Never emit it. The IPA already has the 1600 values; spell those, not today's reading of the same word. meat is meyt, not meet. love and good both have uh. /əɪ/ and /əʊ/ are eye and ow. r is always written.

Map each IPA symbol to the English spelling. Write each word as one string of lowercase letters, capitalized only at the start of a sentence. Stress is not marked: drop ˈ and the syllable dots, and join the syllables of a word.

Vowels
- /iː/ → ee: /ˈmiːt/ is meet.
- /eː/ → ey: /ˈmeːt/ is meyt, /ˈdeː/ is dey, /ˈneː.təɹ/ is neytuhr.
- /ɛ/ → eh
- /ɪ/ → ih, also at the end of a word: /ˈlʊv.lɪ/ is luhvlih.
- /a/ → ae, the vowel of cat: /ˈman/ is maen, /ˈʍat/ is hwaet. Before /ɹ/ in the same syllable, ah: /ˈaɹt/ is ahrt, /ˈdaɹ.lɪn/ is dahrlihn. ae before r would be read as the vowel of air.
- /ɔ/ → aw: /ˈnɔt/ is nawt, /ˈsɔft/ is sawft.
- /ɔː/ → aw: /ˈwɔːk/ is wawk.
- /oː/ → oh: /ˈknoː/ is knoh.
- /uː/ → oo
- /ʊ/ → uh: /ˈlʊv/ is luhv, /ˈblʊd/ is bluhd.
- /ə/ → uh: /ˈsʊ.məɹ/ is suhmuhr. A final /əl/ or /ən/ is uhl or uhn: /ˈnoː.bəl/ is nohbuhl.
- /əɪ/ → eye, the whole nucleus. /ˈknəɪt/ is kneyet, one syllable. /ˈdʒəɪn/ is jeyen.
- /əʊ/ → ow, the whole nucleus. /ˈhəʊ/ is how. /ˈəʊɹ/ is owr, one syllable.
- /ɔɪ/ → oy, the whole nucleus. /ˈbɔɪ/ is boy.
- /ɪu/ → ew, the whole nucleus. /ˈdɪu/ is dew. /ˈdʒɪu.ljɛt/ is jewlyeht.

Vowels before r
- /əɹ/ → uhr: /ˈbəɹd/ is buhrd.
- /iːɹ/ → eer. /eːɹ/ → eyr: /kəmˈpeːɹ/ is kuhmpeyr. /aɹ/ → ahr.
- /ɔːɹ/ → awr: /ˈhɔːɹs/ is hawrs. /oːɹ/ → ohr: /ˈhoːɹs/ is hohrs. Keep these two apart.
- /uːɹ/ → oor. /ɪuɹ/ → ewr.

Consonants
- /p b t d k g f v s z m n l w h/ → the same letters
- /j/ → y
- /θ/ /ð/ → th
- /ʃ/ → sh
- /ʒ/ → zh
- /tʃ/ → ch
- /dʒ/ → j
- /ŋ/ → ng: /ˈsɪŋ/ is sing. /ŋ/ then /g/ in the next syllable → ngg: /ˈfɪŋ.gəɹ/ is fingguhr.
- /ɹ/ → r, including after a vowel and at the end of a word. Do not drop it.
- /ʍ/ → hw: /ˈʍat/ is hwaet.
- /sj/ /tj/ /zj/ → sy, ty, zy: /ˈkwɛs.tjən/ is kwehstyuhn. Never sh or zh.
- /kn/ at the start of a word stays k then n. Do not drop the k. /gn/ and /wr/ likewise keep both consonants.
- /kw/ → kw.
