Stress is already marked, on the first syllable. /h/ at the start of a word stays h. /x/ elsewhere is kh. Do not turn /w/ into v.

Vowels
- /a/ → ah
- /e/ → eh
- /i/ /iː/ → ee
- /u/ → uh
- /uː/ → oo
- /eː/ → ey
- /oː/ → oh
- /ɑi/ → eye
- /ɑu/ → ow
- /eu/ → eh'oo, one syllable
- /iu/ → ee'oo, one syllable
- /ɑ̃/ → ahn, /ĩ/ → een, /ũ/ → oon, each the whole nucleus. That n is nasalization, not an extra /n/.
- /ə/ → uh

Consonants
- /p t k b d g f s z m n l r h/ → the same letters
- /j/ → y
- /w/ → w
- /kʷ/ → kw
- /gʷ/ → gw
- /θ/ → th
- /β/ → v
- /ð/ → th
- /ɣ/ → gh
- /x/ → kh
- /ʍ/ /xʷ/ → hw
- /ŋ/ at the end of a syllable, with /g/ starting the next, is ng at the boundary and g in the next syllable: neeng-gahz.
- /ŋ/ and /g/ in the same syllable are ng, once.
